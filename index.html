01 / About Me

Beyond the Laboratory

A brief introduction to my academic journey, research interests and scientific motivation.

I am a biotechnology student at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, with a growing interest in molecular biology, experimental research and biotechnology applications.

My laboratory experience includes RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, PCR, qPCR, molecular biology techniques and experimental work related to cancer research.

I am particularly interested in understanding the molecular basis of disease and exploring the biological potential of natural products through scientific investigation.

My long-term goal is to develop as an independent researcher and contribute to meaningful scientific discoveries.
